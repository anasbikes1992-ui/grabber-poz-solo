## Executive Direction

Grabber is a productized SME implementation and managed-infrastructure company. The product is **Grabber Business OS Pro**: one complete operating system for POS, inventory, storefront, customers, credit, reports, Jarvis/agents, backups, updates, and support.

We do not sell feature-tier packages. We configure the same Pro product by vertical pack and implementation scope.

## Beachhead

First paid deployments should focus on owner-operated Sri Lankan SMEs with real counter pressure:

- Retail and wholesale merchants.
- Party/event stores with rich product options.
- Electronics/mobile repair shops.
- Small cafes/restaurants only after physical KOT/KDS smoke.

---

## Go-Live Gate

A client is handover-ready only after:

- Production health check passes.
- Owner/admin login and PIN rotation are done.
- POS sale, receipt print, return, stock update, and COD storefront order pass.
- Backup proof and restore procedure exist.
- Provider-dependent claims are proven before being marketed live.
- Seven-day acceptance is signed or explicitly deferred.

---

## Expansion Path

- First 10 clients: prove onboarding, hardware reliability, receipt printing, support load, and retention.
- Next 50 clients: standardize vertical packs, reseller scripts, migration templates, and support playbooks.
- Next 200 clients: scale via repeatable provisioning, case studies, channel partners, and add-on services.

Revenue should come from license/setup, recurring managed infrastructure, implementation scope, vertical configuration, hardware, and AI/media credits — not from withholding core features.
